## The Complete Guide to Cracking Backend Interviews

### Why Backend Matters

Every top company tests backend fundamentals. Not just "build an API." They want you to:
- **Design scalable systems** (APIs, caching, message queues)
- **Understand trade-offs** (SQL vs NoSQL, REST vs GraphQL)
- **Implement security** (authentication, authorization, rate limiting)
- **Optimize performance** (caching strategies, database indexing)
- **Handle distributed challenges** (eventual consistency, message ordering)
