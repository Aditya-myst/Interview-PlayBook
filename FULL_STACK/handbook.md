# Full Stack Engineering Interview Handbook

## The Complete Guide to Cracking Full Stack Interviews

---

### Why Full Stack Matters

Every top company tests full stack fundamentals. Not just "build an app." They want you to:
- **Understand the full picture** (client ↔ server ↔ database)
- **Build real applications** (API integration, auth flows)
- **Deploy to production** (Docker, CI/CD, cloud)
- **Monitor and debug** (logging, metrics, alerts)
- **Think about scalability** (caching, load balancing, horizontal scaling)

---

### What Interviewers Actually Ask

They won't ask "What is HTTP?" That's too basic.

They'll ask:

> "Walk me through what happens when a user logs in."
> "How would you deploy this application to production?"
> "How do you handle environment variables securely?"
> "Explain the authentication flow from frontend to backend."
> "How would you set up CI/CD for this project?"
> "How do you monitor a production application?"
> "Explain client-server architecture and the request lifecycle."
> "How would you containerize this application with Docker?"

**This book prepares you for those questions.**
