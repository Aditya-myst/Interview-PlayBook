# 🚀 The 30-Day 80/20 Interview System

> **Goal:** Crack Software Development Engineer (SDE), Full-Stack, and AI Engineering interviews at top companies in just **30 days** by focusing on the highest ROI topics.

---

# Why this roadmap?

Most interview roadmaps try to teach **everything**.

That's the wrong approach.

Companies don't hire people because they know every algorithm or every design pattern.

They hire people who consistently solve common interview problems, communicate well, and understand software engineering fundamentals.

This roadmap follows the **Pareto Principle (80/20 Rule)**:

> **20% of the topics appear in nearly 80% of interviews.**

Instead of learning hundreds of random concepts, we'll master the ones that matter the most.

# Core Principles

## 1. Consistency beats intensity.

No 12-hour study marathons.

Show up every day.

---

## 2. Solve fewer problems, understand them deeply.

Instead of solving 500 questions...

Understand why 150 questions work.

---

## 3. Revision is mandatory.

Learning happens during revision—not while watching videos.

---

## 4. Build communication skills.

A perfect solution explained poorly is still a weak interview.

Every problem should be explained aloud.

---

## 5. Projects matter.

You should be able to defend every line of code you've written.

---

# Weekly Schedule

## Monday – Thursday

### 🧠 DSA (Primary Focus)

* Solve **5 interview-quality problems**
* Explain every solution
* Analyze:

  * Time Complexity
  * Space Complexity
  * Edge Cases
  * Better approaches

Approx: **3–4 hours**

---

### 🏗️ System Design

Spend **1 hour** learning.

Alternate between:

* Low Level Design
* High Level Design

Focus on fundamentals instead of memorizing interviews.

---

### 💼 Project Deep Dive

Spend **30–45 minutes**

Practice explaining:

* Architecture
* Tradeoffs
* APIs
* Database
* Authentication
* Challenges
* Scaling
* Future improvements

---

### 🎤 HR / Behavioral

Spend **10–20 minutes**

Practice:

* Tell me about yourself
* Why should we hire you?
* Strengths
* Weaknesses
* Biggest challenge
* Leadership
* Conflict
* Failure
* Resume walkthrough

---

## Friday – Sunday

### 🔁 DSA Revision

No new questions.

Revise:

* 5 previous questions
* Explain from memory
* Re-code without looking
* Recall patterns

Goal:

```
Friday
5 revisions

Saturday
5 revisions

Sunday
5 revisions
```

---

### 🧠 CS Fundamentals Revision

Rotate between:

* Operating Systems
* DBMS
* SQL
* Computer Networks
* OOP

---

### 🏗️ System Design Revision

Revise notes.

No binge watching.

---

### 🎤 Mock Interview

One complete interview simulation.

Explain everything verbally.

# Success Metrics

By Day 30, you should be able to:

* Solve medium-level DSA problems confidently.
* Explain your solution before writing code.
* Design basic scalable systems.
* Discuss your projects in depth.
* Answer common CS theory questions.
* Handle behavioral interviews using the STAR method.
* Write clean, production-style code.
* Think aloud like an engineer.

---

# Golden Rule

> **Don't study to finish the roadmap.**

> **Study until you can teach the topic to someone else.**

If you can explain it simply, solve it confidently, and defend your decisions—you are interview-ready.

Let's build that version of ourselves, one day at a time. 🚀
